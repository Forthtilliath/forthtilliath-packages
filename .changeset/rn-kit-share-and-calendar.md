New helpers: `shareFile` / `shareTextFile` (`utils/helpers/`, native share sheet, optionally writing the text to the cache first) and `syncDedicatedCalendar` / `toCalendarEventDetails` (`utils/calendar/`, replace a period's events in the app's own local calendar, with Android-safe all-day events and night shifts). `expo-sharing`, `expo-file-system` and `expo-calendar` are optional peer dependencies.
