Six new themes under `styles/themes/`, each with its own fonts, corner radius and shadows rather than just another color: `brutalist`, `terminal`, `editorial`, `aurora`, `nordic`, `forest`. Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA (4.5:1) in light and dark mode. Their fonts fall back to system stacks — load the fonts named at the top of each file for the full look.
