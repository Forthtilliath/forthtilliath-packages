New components, each with neutral defaults and per-slot `styles` overrides: `BottomSheet` (`components/overlay/`), `ErrorFallback` (`components/feedback/`), `AddButton` and `DragHandle` (`components/list/`), `OptionsModal`, `SelectField`, `SegmentedToggle`, `QuantityStepper` and `MonthDayGrid` (`components/picker/`), and `ThemePreferenceProvider` with `useThemePreference` / `useResolvedScheme` (`components/theme/`). `SwipeableRow` gains `fullSwipeToDelete` (swipe past half the row to delete right away, no button) and a `container` style slot.
